SI(t) carries a circadian rhythm and a post-exercise boost; Gb(t) carries the dawn phenomenon. SGLT2 inhibitors lower the renal threshold. In the simulator (not the twin), sleep debt, stress, illness and missed doses also modulate SI and Gb; the ML layer learns those from HRV, heart rate and sleep.

How each claim is tested

  1. Prediction (held-out synthetic). 45 unseen virtual patients, days 8–21, every 5 min. AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, reliability curve.
  2. Alert usefulness. One alert policy (≤1/hour, ≥9 of 12 readings present) shared with the console. For every excursion: was a delivered alert raised in the prior 2 h (one-to-one matching), and how early? False alerts and precision from the same alerts.
  3. Fusion value. Ablation: EHR only, wearables only, fusion, fusion + twin; plus a cold-start test for the first 48 h of a new patient.
  4. Twin fidelity (synthetic). Each virtual patient's hidden physiology is known; we report rank correlations with the truth. Simulator and Twin share equations, so this is an upper bound; a model-mismatch stress test changes the generator's physics.
  5. What-if accuracy (synthetic). Interventions are re-run on the hidden virtual patient and compared with the Twin. Under the stress test, rankings held but effect sizes were biased; real-patient effects are unvalidated.
  6. Retrospective open cohorts. 265 adults (Hall 2018; Colas 2019; only 10 with T2D), CGM + labs only. Pipeline re-trained with patient-level CV, target ≥140 mg/dL: CGM-only and CGM + EHR reported separately, plus SSPG insulin-resistance checks.
  7. Leakage guards. Patient-level split (210 train / 45 validation / 45 test; no person in two sets) and temporal split (twin fitted on days 1–7, evaluated on days 8–21). Every feature uses data at or before the decision time; personal baselines use completed nights only; habits come from week 1.

India-context design choices (synthetic simulation)

  1. Asian BMI and waist cut-offs. (Genotype is in the record but adds no predictive value, so the risk model excludes it.)
  2. 37-item regional food library (idli, poha, rajma chawal, fish-rice, millets) with carbs and GI.
  3. Late dinners, sweet chai, festival thalis, office commutes, weekend drift.
  4. Metformin, sulfonylurea, DPP-4i, SGLT2i and basal insulin mix typical of Indian practice (in the record; SGLT2i what-if kept as a research option, not in the demo).
  5. Built to run on modest hardware and offline, for tier-2/3 clinics.

Privacy and governance layer

  1. No real patient data in this repo. Synthetic cohort + CC-BY de-identified open cohorts only.
  2. Consent (DPDP Act 2023). Purpose-bound consent per data stream (CGM, watch, food log, EHR); revocable from the patient app; ABDM consent artefacts.
  3. Minimisation. The twin needs 5-min aggregates, not raw accelerometry; no location, no free text leaves the device.
  4. Security. Encryption at rest and in transit; role-based access; audit log of every view and alert acknowledgement.
  5. Human in the loop. Advisory only: alerts go to the care team; no automatic dosing; every suggestion shows its reasons and confidence.
  6. Fairness monitoring (proposed). AUROC with CIs by sex, age, BMI, HbA1c, region and medication; a release gate if any subgroup drops is a proposed governance step.
  7. Model card (docs/model_card.md): intended use, out-of-scope use, data, metrics, limits.

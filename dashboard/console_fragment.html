          What the patient sees same twin, plain words

          The doctor's alert becomes a gentle nudge on the patient's phone, in English or Hindi, timed before the usual meal. More Indian languages can be added from a strings table.

          Design rules: one message at a time, no numbers the patient has to interpret, always one doable action, and an escalation path ("call your doctor") for sick days. Notification wording is reviewed by the treating clinician.
